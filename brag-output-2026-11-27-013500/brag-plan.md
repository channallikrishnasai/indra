# /brag plan: INDRA climate digital twin of India

Output: brag-output-2026-11-27-013500/brag.mp4 (landscape 1920x1080, 30 fps, 20 s).
Tone: polished, cinematic motion-design look, honest. Direction: a motion-designer film that stays
truthful about limits: show the real dashboard, real numbers, and say what it can not do.

## Angle
Climate is a single future; INDRA runs the monsoon forward in five futures and says where they agree.

## Hook (0-2.6 s)
"Where will the monsoon go in ten days?" then "Five futures. One model."
