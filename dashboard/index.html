    About the model

    How INDRA works

    A small neural network that learned India’s weather from 16 years of daily data, then rolls it forward day by day, and lets you change the start to see an alternate future.

    1. 1Reads 3 daysTemperature, rain, wind, pressure and moisture on a 32 × 36 grid over India (1° ≈ 110 km).
    2. 2Predicts tomorrowA CNN looks at each place and its neighbours, 7 cells around, and predicts the change.
    3. 3Repeats for 10 daysIt feeds its own forecast back in. Calibration gently pulls far days towards normal weather.
    4. 4Branches universesWith a shock it runs both starts and adds the full difference, so you see exactly what the shock caused.

    Model card

    Main model
    Residual CNN, 534,726 weights
    Second model
    INDRA-Risk CNN, 28,274 weights: chance of heavy rain and heat stress
    Trained on
    2005–2020 daily data; 2021 kept aside for testing
    Training
    1-day steps, then 2, 4 and 6 days in a row so it learns to fix its own drift
    Safety rules
    Rain and moisture never below 0; temperature stays within −40…+60 °C; a zero shock changes nothing
    Runs
    Fully offline on CPU; the only stored table is the 2005–2020 average weather per calendar day
